Privacy

Hot Wheels Catalog Browser (diecastDb) · Last updated 30 October 2026

In short: this site has no server and no database. If you sign in with Google, your collection and wishlist are saved in your own Google Drive, in a hidden folder that only this app can open. We never receive or see your data.

Browsing

You can browse the catalog and use the card scanner without signing in. The scanner reads the photo on your device; the photo isn't uploaded. The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies.

Signing in with Google

Sign-in is optional and only used for your collection and wishlist. The site asks Google for one permission, drive.appdata: "See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive". With it, the site can only use its own hidden app folder. It can't see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

To show who is signed in, the site reads your name and email address from Google Drive. They are kept in your browser only.

What is stored

  • In your Google Drive (hidden app folder): one file, diecastdb-lists.json, with the cars in your collection and wishlist. For each car it holds the catalog id, year, collection, name and the time you added it.
  • In your browser (local storage): a copy of that list, your name and email, so the lists show right away and changes made offline aren't lost. The Google access token is kept only for the current tab session and expires within an hour.

Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's. The data is not shared, sold or used for advertising.

Telegram

The Telegram Mini App version is browse-only. It has no sign-in and doesn't store a collection or wishlist.

Deleting your data

  • In the app, tap the account button, then Delete my data. This removes the file from your Drive and the copy in your browser.
  • Or, in Google Drive on the web: Settings → Manage apps → diecastDb → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • To remove the app's access, go to myaccount.google.com/connections. Sign out in the app also revokes access and clears the copy in your browser.

You can download your lists at any time with Export (JSON).
